About Me

Where Rigorous Data Science

I operate at the critical interface between raw data and intelligent systems. My work spans the full lifecycle: cleaning and structuring messy datasets, designing RLHF pipelines that teach models to prefer accurate over plausible, and stress-testing language model outputs against adversarial edge cases.

Every data artefact I produce is built to be reproducible, traceable, and high-fidelity. I don't ship datasets—I ship truth.
